SECTION: SERVICE PRICING TIERS (Effective January 2025)
-------------------------------------------------------

Atlas offers three core engagement tiers:

1. Discovery (£5,000)
   - Duration: 2 weeks
   - Includes: stakeholder interviews, current-state assessment,
     opportunity identification report
   - Deliverable: Discovery Summary Report (15-20 pages)
   - Suitable for: clients exploring digital transformation options

2. Strategy (£15,000)
   - Duration: 6-8 weeks
   - Includes: full process mapping, technology assessment, vendor
     evaluation, transformation roadmap with prioritised initiatives
   - Deliverable: Digital Transformation Strategy Document (40-60 pages)
   - Suitable for: clients ready to plan a transformation programme

3. Implementation (from £35,000)
   - Duration: 3-12 months depending on scope
   - Includes: hands-on delivery, change management, vendor coordination,
     training, and post-go-live support (first 30 days)
   - Deliverable: deployed solution with documentation and training pack
   - Pricing varies by scope; quoted after Strategy phase completion

Bundled pricing: clients who commit to Discovery + Strategy together
receive a 10% discount (combined price: £18,000 instead of £20,000).

SECTION: STANDARD RESPONSE TIMES (SLA)
---------------------------------------

All client emails must receive a substantive response within 24 hours
(one business day). Automated acknowledgements do not count.

- Urgent emails (flagged or containing keywords: "urgent", "escalate",
  "deadline", "legal"): target response within 4 hours during business
  hours (Mon-Fri, 09:00-17:30 GMT).
- Non-urgent general enquiries: within 24 hours.
- New client enquiries: within 8 business hours (prioritised).

SECTION: MEETING BOOKING PROCESS
---------------------------------

To book a meeting with a client or prospect:
1. Check consultant availability on the shared Outlook calendar.
2. Propose 2-3 time slots spanning at least two different days.
3. All meetings must include a brief agenda (minimum 3 bullet points).
4. Send calendar invites with a Teams link (default) or Zoom if the
   client requests it.
5. For first meetings with new prospects, CC the Business Development
   lead (currently: Emma Richardson, e.richardson@atlaspro.co.uk).
